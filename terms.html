1. Terms of use

The Tahfeez Daressalaam portal ("the portal") is run by Qism al Tahfeez, Dar es Salaam ("the institute") for its students, their parents or guardians, its teachers and its staff. By signing in you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the portal and speak to the institute.

  • Accounts are personal. Usernames and passwords are given by the institute to one person only. Do not share them, and do not let anyone else sign in as you.
  • Use it for its purpose. The portal is for hifz progress, schedules, attendance, reports, notices and fees of the institute. Do not try to read or change anything that is not yours, to get around a lock or limit, to test its security without written permission, or to slow it down.
  • Be truthful and respectful. Enter only correct information. Feedback about teachers must be honest and respectful.
  • What you see is the institute’s record. Pages, marks, attendance and reports are recorded by the institute’s staff. If you think something is wrong, ask the institute to check it.
  • Changes and access. The institute may change the portal or these terms, and may pause or close an account that is misused or no longer needed. The date at the top shows the latest version.

2. Privacy

The institute keeps only what it needs to teach, care for and look after its students and staff, and handles personal data in line with the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 of Tanzania.

What is kept

  • Student details given at admission: name, program, date of birth, gender, ITS number, parent or guardian name, phone and email.
  • Learning records: pages memorised, monthly goals and reports, ikhtibar, keys and badges, schedules and attendance.
  • Fee records and receipts, and payment slips if they are uploaded.
  • Staff records needed to run the institute (for example teacher details, ratings and payroll).
  • Technical records needed to keep the portal safe, such as sign-in times.

Who can see it

  • A student sees only their own records.
  • A teacher sees only the students linked to them, and never fees.
  • Accounts staff see fees and the money records they need.
  • The admin of the institute sees everything needed to run it.
  • Feedback from students about teachers is shown to teachers without names.

The institute does not sell personal data or use it for advertising. The portal does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It uses trusted services to work: Supabase (secure database and sign-in), Vercel (website hosting) and an email service to send messages the institute chooses to send. These services keep the data safe for the institute and do not use it for their own purposes.

How long and your choices

Records are kept while a student studies at the institute and afterwards for as long as needed for certificates, fee records and the institute’s legal duties. A student, parent or guardian may ask the institute to see the records kept, to correct anything wrong, or to delete what is no longer needed, where the law allows.

3. Children and parents

Many students are under 18. Their accounts are created by the institute with the knowledge of their parent or guardian, who may see the student’s records with them. Parents and guardians can contact the institute at any time about their child’s data.

4. Security

  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS) and browsers are told to use only secure connections.
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text and are never visible to staff.
  • Every record is protected in the database itself by strict per-person rules, so a person can only reach what their role allows, whatever the screen shows.
  • Changes to important records (for example pages, attendance and schedules) are logged with who made them and when.
  • Staff accounts are signed out automatically after a period of no activity.
  • Please use a strong password, sign out on shared phones or computers, and tell the institute at once if you think someone else has used your account.

5. Emails and phone alerts

The institute may send emails about lessons, timetables, reports, notices and fees to the email address saved for you. Phone alerts are sent only if you switch them on, and you can switch them off at any time in your browser or phone settings.

6. Fees

Fees, discounts and due dates are set by the institute. Receipts in the portal are the institute’s record of payments received. If a payment is missing or wrong, contact the institute’s accounts office with your receipt or slip.

7. Contact

For any question about these terms, your data or your account, please contact the office of Qism al Tahfeez, Dar es Salaam.
